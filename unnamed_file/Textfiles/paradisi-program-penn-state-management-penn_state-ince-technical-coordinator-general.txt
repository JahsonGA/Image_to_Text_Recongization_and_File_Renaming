Morning Call Intern

Romulo Paradisi, from Caracas,
Venezuela, and President Carter have
something in common — both are
involved in supplying American ex-

pertise to the South American country.

Officials of the government of Vene-
zuela and Pennsylvania State Univer-
sity recently signed a five-year agree-
ment under which personnel of the
university will assist Venezuela in an
extensive program to provide occupa-
tional and vocational training to that
nation’s working class. |

It was that agreement which
brought Paradisi to Pennsylvania and
to the Lehigh Valley.

The program is to teach personnel
of INCE how to apply, adapt and
modify methods, techniques and pro-
cedures used by Penn State in its
continuing education program.

Penn State conducts tailor-made
courses for any organization or compa-
ny interested in bettering its man-
agement.

Representatives of the university
study the organization, interview every.
potential student, and administer eval-
uative tests.

One of 12 professors, all specialists
in general management, then conducts
a 10-30 week management course at the
place of business.

Paradisi, technical coordinator of
general management at INCE, wants
to apply the concept of management-
in-plant to programs in Venezuela.

“INCE provides many services to
